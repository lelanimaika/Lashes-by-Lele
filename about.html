ABOUT US

Lashes by Lele is a lash beauty business based in Tlokweng, Gaborone. It was started in late 2024 by a young dreamer, Lelani Maika, professionally known as "Lele", whose main goal is to enhance the already beautiful faces of women of all age groups.

We offer classic, hybrid and volume lash styles to help clients express their beauty and confidence.
